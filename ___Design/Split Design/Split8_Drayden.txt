/*   PLASMA FRIGATE   */
    
    This is the first infiltration of the Plasma Frigate. You will board the frigate, it will shake, and it will take off with you on it. 

    I believe you explore a lot of it with Hugh, so this can be another "tag or back to back" situation. 

    There will also be a "dogpile" battle with a field effect that drains the player's Pokemon's HP. 

    You will not be able to travel off the ship. If you white out you will respawn not at the last pokemon center. 

    Tbh we need to look at how this is CURRENTLY scripted.
