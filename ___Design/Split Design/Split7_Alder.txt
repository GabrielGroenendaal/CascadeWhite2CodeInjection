
    
    
This split begins after defeating Skyla, and has a level cap of 59. Within this split is the following content: 
    - Reversal Mountain (rigorous route with trainers with sun teams) 
    - Strange House (optional back to back to back with heals in between for an encounter)
    - Route 14, a gauntlet 
    - Undella Town, where there is an optional Blaine battle to get an encounter. 
    - Virizion Side Quest (Optional)
    - Final Alder battle.
