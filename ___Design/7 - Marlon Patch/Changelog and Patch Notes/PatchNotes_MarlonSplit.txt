``[ NEW CHANGES ]``
> - **Effect Spore** is now an AI only ability. It triggers in response to any move, contact or otherwise, and has an 50% proc chance. It won't affect Grass types, pokemon with Overcoat, or Pokemon holding Safety Goggles.
> - **Cursed Body** now has a 60% proc chance. 
> - **AI Switch Logic** accounts for Tera when applying the STAB bonus for switch in. 
> - **Enforced Level Caps** are now a toggle-able option in the PokeHelper menu. While in the PokeHelper menu, you can also see the current level cap.
> - **Switch AI** now takes in defensive matchups into consideration as a BP modifier. It checks the types of the pokemon it's calculating BP against to see if either is super effective or 4x effective against the switch in Pokemon, applying a 0.75x or 0.5x modifier accordingly. If the Pokemon resists or is immune to both types, they can get a 1.25x or 1.5x modifier. 
> - **Shadow Triad** no longer steal a random Pokemon. Instead they steal the Pokemon in the party with the lowest health that isn't fainted. Hugh will now HEAL you before the battle! You can't get it to specific values, but to 1/5/10/20/50/100/150/200/250 HP.
