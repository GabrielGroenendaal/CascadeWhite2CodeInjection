/*   NEW PROGRESSION   */


This is the new progression for Kyurem Split Plasma Frigate.


    [ BATTLE IN OPELUCID ]
        There are now three sets of tag battles here. 
        After team plasma successfully steals the Dark Stone. 
        Iris will leave to go find the other legendary dragon to defeat Kyurem. 

    [ RUMBLE IN THE GIANT CHASM ]
        A 6v20 Rumble in the Giant Chasm outside the Plasma Frigate. 

    [ TELEPORTER PUZZLE ]
        Pretty much vanilla, but we're going to need to change some more trainers to be mandatory. 

    [ ZINZOLIN BOSS ]
        This is now a single battle with Zinzolin. 
        We need to edit this script so that Kyurem jumps out of it's enclosure again. 
        Afterwards, Zinzolin will withdraw Kyurem and deliver it to Ghetsis while the player has to go content with Colress. 

    [ COLRESS BATTLE ]
        Nothing to change here
        Interacting with an item in the back of his room spawns a wild Pokemon. 

    [ SHADOW TRIAD BATTLE ]
        You are ambushed by one member of the Shadow Triad. 
        Two more show up and Janine appears to assist. 
        After they are defeated, they will try to escape only to realize they are paralyzed
        Janine reveals she nicked them with her family's poison while they were distracted by the battle. 
        The triad will fall to the floor 
        Janine will thank the player for their aid. She's not sure she could've managed it by herself. 
        What will she do with them now? Bring them back to Kanto for justice. 
        She'll give the player something spicy here. 

    [ GHETSIS ]
        You pursue Ghetsis into the back of Giant Chasm. 
        When you encounter Ghetsis, his attack is interrupted by N & Reshiram (?*)
        N will mention Iris and the other gym leaders are already busy rounding up the grunts outside. 
        N will battle Kyurem while Ghetsis battles you. 

        *part of me thinks that these beats would work better if it's Zekrom and Kyurem-Black, since it better fits the idea of darkness and 

    [ POST GHETSIS SCENE ]
        After Ghetsis is defeated he will go fufufufu 
        He commands Kyurem to fuse with Reshiram/Zekrom. The cutscene happens. 
        Afterwards, instead of obeying, Kyurem goes into a frenzy and N tackles the player to get them out of the way of an attack. 
        The player is sent back to the title screen. 

    [ POST FUSION SCENE ]
        The player awakes in a completely frozen version of kyurem's lair with massive ice crystals and a mist rolling over everything. 
        Ghetsis is frozen in a block of ice. 
        The player can retrieve the DNA splicers from his staff. 
        N remarks that the only reason Kyurem did not freeze them is because there's probably something left of Reshiram in there 
        But that it's being smothered by Kyurem's rage and sorrow. 
        N notes that it took the Dark stone with it... does it hope to fuse with Zekrom too...?
        N will accompany the player as they leave. 

    [ GIANT CHASM ]
        The plasma frigate is gone from Giant Chasm. Team Plasma left, as Colress said they would. 
        N remarks that they can hear the sounds of battle from the center of the crater... is that Iris? 
        The anguish of Pokemon is all N can hear. It's defeaning. 

        The chasm itself has a unique weather effect that is like pitch darkness + howling winds. 

    [ GIANT CHASM ENTITIES ]
        Giant Chasm is filled with roaming Wild Pokemon that use the Trainer Encounter scripts. 
        There are four "quadrants" each of which can be cleared by a sword of justice you helped. 
        These wild Pokemon are level 70 and receive an omniboost.

        Ideally, there is a unique theme for the wild pokemon if fought here. 
        They wiil also have unique sprites. 

        Shivering Plasma Grunts can be found around hiding from their now berserk pokemon. 
        Benga, Hugh, Roood can be encountered around fending off crazed Pokemon. 

    [ GIANT CHASM FINALE ]
        Kyurem stands in the middle of the chasm, having just beaten Iris down. 
        It seems heavily damaged from the battle.
        Kyurem has a special triples battle with a unique mechanic. 
        After Kyurem is defeated, you unfuse it and Reshiram. 
        The weather changes to not shit, maybe the "diamond dust" effect?
        N gives his usual spiel, and says Kyurem was a hollow creature filled with the hate and cruelty of humans. Perhaps now it needs time to rest and return to a neutral state. 
        Iris thanks the player, and invites them to seek out the Pokemon League. 
        Around the chasm Benga and Hugh will give the players words of encouragement.
