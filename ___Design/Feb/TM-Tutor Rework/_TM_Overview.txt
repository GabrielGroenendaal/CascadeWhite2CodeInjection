



Implementing this change to the Tutors and TMs will involve: 
    - Editing the TM Table 
    - Editing the Tutor Tables 
    - Editing the comptability of tutor/tm moves for all Pokemon. 
    - Adding up to 4 new trainers. 

The Main questions I have to answer regarding the TMs is: 
    - Are there any TMs i value less than a potential Payback or Beat Up TM. 
    - How should low accuracy TMs be handled (personally i like removing compatibility for the mons that abuse it like no guard mons)
