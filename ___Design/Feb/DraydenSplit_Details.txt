    Lacunosa Town:

    Route 12: 
        - Nothing to do here but add the new encounters and block off the hidden grotto.
    
    Village Bridge: 
        - This is the next big scripting task
        - We are going to make a big gauntlet here, six fights minimum. 
        - There is an optional fantina fight in the mix as well. 
    
    Route 11: 
        - This is pretty tame, we're just going to have a single scientist guarding the doubles grass encounter that is very easy to set up. 
        - We're also going to remove the virizion encounter here. 
    
    Opelucid City: 
        - We're going to alter the dialogue here to support the idea that the protagonist of BW entrusted the white stone to Drayden to safeguard. 
        - We're going to add the back to back battle with Tate and Liza here. 
        - We need to have Iris point the player towards Twist Mountain.
    
    Route 9: 
        - We're going to add the optional Blue battle here. Defeating him will let him teleport you into the Challenger's Cave. 
        - We're going to set up the Hooligans as the gatekeeper here and an optional for a TM. 
    
    SM9: 
        - Optional Whitney Fight 
        - We need to edit the inventories here, and we also need to add gatekeepers for some of the vendors here that only unlock with specific items acquired from boss battles. 
    
    Twist Mountain: 
        - Short scripted sections with Iris at twist mountain. 
        - Terrakion is here on a rampage because of the construction disrupting the pokemon's natural habitat. 
        - Add two optional mini boss battles against Roark and Byron here. 
    
    Opelucid Gym: 
        - Add an intermediary room between the entrance and the gym proper where we can put a few more trainers. 
        - We need to implement the new field effect but it should be pretty simple; just copying the effects for Anger Point. 
        - there will be six mandatory fights here, although there will be a choice for the last two and the first one.
