
This split begins after defeating Skyla, and has a level cap of 59. Within this split is the following content: 
    - Reversal Mountain (rigorous route with trainers that can be fought as Tag Battles or b2b battles) 
    - Strange House (optional back to back to back with heals in between for an encounter)
    - Route 14, a gauntlet 
    - Undella Town, where there is a rough tag battle vs. Alder
    - Abundant Shrine (optional), gatekept by the Kanto leader Blaine
    - Marine Tube (rigorous battle route with the Shadow Traid ambushing you)
    - Virizion Side Quest (Optional)
    - Humilau City and Gym, which caps off this split 
    - Optional Gatekeeper bosses guarding Route 22 and Route 21. 

Encounters:
    Mandatory: 
        Lentimas Town
        Reversal Mountain 
        Route 14 
        Humilau Town 
        Route 21
        Route 22
    Optional:
        Abundant Shrine
        Pinwheel Forest
        Strange House

Bosses:
    Strange House Gauntlet 
    Route 14 Gauntlet 
    Alder & Victini
    Marine Tube Gauntlet
    Blaine
    Virizion
    Marlon
