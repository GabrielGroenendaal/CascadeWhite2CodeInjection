- Fixed the text when receiving a trade saying "Welcome Back, Pokemon!"

**Balance**: 
- Seadra can now evolve to Kingdra through use of a Dawn Stone 
- Onix now evolves to Steelix through use of a Shiny Stone
- Scyther now evolves into Scizor through use of a Shiny Stone 
- Spinda now learns Dizzy Punch at 31, not Feint Attack 
- Whiscash's Ability 1 is now Mold Breaker instead of Adaptability 
- Emolga now learns Aerial Ace at 17, not Air Cutter 
- Sandile now evolves into Krokorok at level 14, and Krokorok evolves into Krookadile at level 30.
- Staravia gains +20 HP, +15 Atk, +10 Special Defense. It now learns Submission at 13 and Retaliate at 17.
- Buizel gains +10 Atk, Special Attack, and Speed. 
- Buizel's Ability 1 is now Torrent, which becomes Reckless on Evolution. 
- Buizel/Floatzel now have a revamped early game learnset (Level 4 Detect, Level 14 U-Turn,etc.)
- Skitty/Delcatty have a slightly revamped early game learnset (level 16 Secret Power, level 12 Doubleslap, etc.)
- Tepig/Pignite/Emboar have a slightly revamped learnset, gaining Stompin Tantrum for Cheren Split and replacing Flamethrower with Heat Wave. 
- Bulbasaur Line now learns Mega Drain instead of Absorb at level 5. 
- Bulbasaur Line now has reduced attack, increased defense. 
- Bulbasaur Line abilities are now Thick Fat / Overgrow / Huge Power


**Trainers**
- Cheren's Skitty is now level 13, and his Unova Starters are level 14.
- Scientist Terrance's Kirlia has had their ability fixed to Pixilate 
- Ranger Jaiden's Mandibuzz has their ability fixed to Overcoat. It now has Swords Dance and a Wacan Berry 
- Lt. Surge's Raichu now has Sludge Bomb over Strength 
- Burgh's Gliscor now has Dig, as it works properly with trapping in doubles now. 
- Youngster Pedro's Sentret is now Run Away instead of Adaptability. Secret Power has been replaced with Retaliate.
- Partner Hugh's Pignite in Castelia Sewers now has Heat Wave instead of Flamethrower. 
- Ranger Serenity's Tangrowth now has Thick Fat as intended.
