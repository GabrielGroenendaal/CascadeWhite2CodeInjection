# Cascade White

Thank you for playing Cascade White! A brief overview of major changes not immediately obvious in this mastersheet is provided below.

### Mechanics Changes 
Besides the trainer, encounter, and Pokémon changes displayed in this master sheet, there are countless changes to moves, abilities, and items in Cascade White that go beyond simple BP tweaks, including brand new mechanics altogether. Rivalry now increases damage against and reduces damage taken from Pokémon who share a type. Stockpile, Spitup and Swalllow now consume the user's held berry to activate. Gastro Acid now has +1 Priority, Zap Cannon is a recoil move, Run Away now has the effect of Emergency Exit and COUNTLESS other changes. 
These changes are all indicated in in-game text, so make sure to read carefully! But also make sure to use the following resources!
- The Cascade White Changes (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1MQ5JODn_IfyXb26u8rTx2yXfOFiEnTBukeLtgtGJPBw/edit?usp=sharing) sheet documents all changes to Pokémon, Items, Abilities, and Moves. 
- The Official Cascade White Mastersheet (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1QGKvMsYrywhhnhpLKbTL-kCA0VJZ_zXj-dI2rcTw9ns/edit?usp=sharing) is a version of the mastersheet that includes notation for majorly changed moves, abilities, and items for your convenience. 

### AI Changes 
The AI in Cascade White has been updated to provide a more engaging gameplay experience. These changes are not as extensive as something like Ancestral X, but there have been substantial changes to switch-in AI and moderate changes to AI Scoring for status, setup, and priority moves. 
A brief overview of the changes is: 
- The AI is not guaranteed to go for priority when it sees kill if it's already faster than all Pokémon on the field. 
- The AI has a very high chance to use a Priority move when it is not the fastest Pokémon on the field and an enemy Pokémon sees kill on it
- The AI is slightly encouraged to go for setup moves when it sees enemy Pokémon deal negligible (25% or less) damage to it.
- The AI is heavily discouraged from using setup moves when an enemy Pokémon sees a OHKO on it, or a 2HKO if the enemy mon is faster and the setup move does not increase speed. 
- The AI is discouraged from using stat-lowering moves when the target is dealing negligible (25% or less) damage to it.
- The AI is heavily discouraged from using status or stat-lowering moves into targets holding the respective curative items. 
- The AI no longer receives a negative score with damaging moves that lower speed when they are faster than the target.
- When determining BP for Switch AI, the AI is able to account for STAB, move effects that alter BP, moves and abilities that alter type effectiveness (Freeze Dry, Corrosion), abiities that provide resistances and immunities, and abilities that can alter BP such as Technician and Skill Link. 
- When simulating damage for moves, the AI can now see the correct damage for moves with variable BP (Hex, Electro Ball, Flail, etc.)
- The AI has a very low chance to switch out mid-turn if it is dealing negligible damage to all Pokémon on the field. This chance increases each turn the Pokémon is in battle. 
- The AI now has a high chance to switch out mid-turn if it is locked into a move that deals low damage via Encore or Choice Items.
A full list of the AI Changes is outlined in the AI Changes Overview(https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z2hoQeMt998p8sx8VA0MbgYViWAQwedM/view?usp=sharing) document. 

### Quality of Life Improvements
Many changes have been made to the game to improve quality of life and provide options for customizing the player's gameplay experience. 

#### PokéHelper
The biggest addition is that of the PokéHelper, a new piece of technology available from the start of the game that can be access by pressing the START button on your device. This opens up a menu where you can: 
- Access your PC
- Heal your Party
- Apply Status to your Party 
- Edge Party Exp
- Remove Party Held Items
- Toggle Permanent Repel
- Fast Travel (unlocked after defeating Hugh in Virbank Gate)
- Change Settings (see below)
The PokéHelper can be utilized anywhere except for when participating in a Trainer Gauntlet, where all functions will be disabled. Make sure to stock up on Healing items!

#### Settings 
You are now able to customize your gameplay through the Settings Menu in the PokeHelper 
- Critical Hit Damage (Toggle): Critical Hits deal 2x / 1.5x damage (default: 2x)
- Animated Backgrounds (Toggle): The game does/doesn't use the new animated backgrounds in battle (default: doesn't) Note that these backgrounds are not fully tested and can cause crashes or visual glitches. 
- IV Randomization: when generating IVs for Wild Pokémon, they have random/improved/boosted/perfect IVs. (default: random) "improved" rolls IVs twice and uses the higher total. "boosted" sets three random ivs to 31. "perfect" sets all ivs to 31.
- EVs: Defeating Pokémon rewards no/1x/2x/4x/8x EVs. (default: no EVs)
- Advanced Switch AI (Toggle): The AI does/doesn't use the advanced mid-turn switch AI (default: does)
- Trainer Placements (Toggle): The game uses the modified/vanilla placements and movement for trainers (default: modified) This setting reverts Trainers to vanilla-like positions and movement, making the majority of them optional again. Good for casual play!
- Battle Scan Detail (Toggle): The Battle Scan feature reveals partial/complete information about enemy Pokémon. (default: partial) "partial" info displays the Pokémon's ability, base stats, typing, and any moves that have been revealed in battle. It hides items, raw stats, and any team members who have not yet entered battle. "complete" info displays all the above and also raw stat values, held item, all moves, and all Pokémon on the team. 

#### Battle Scan
The second major quality-of-life improvement is the addition of Battle Scan, which allows you to view detailed information about opponent Pokémon and teams in battle. 
In Battle, simply hold the [RIGHT BUMPER] or [LEFT BUMPER] and then Press the Pokémon Button and you can view a summary of the opponent trainer's team. In battles against multiple trainers, [LEFT BUMPER] will examine the left Trainer and [RIGHT BUMPER] will examine the right Trainer.
Depending on your configured settings (accessed in the PokéHelper), you will be able to view...
- Base / Raw Stats 
- Typing 
- Held Item 
- Ability 
- Moves 
...for all Pokémon in battle. 

#### Miscellaneous
Other miscellaneous quality-of-life changes include the following: 
- HMs are now enabled at specific points in the story when your PokéHelper is upgraded, and you do not require a Pokémon to know the move. 
- Rare Candies are now re-usable.
- Unusual Candy is a new item that edges your Pokémon to 1 exp of the next level. Reusable.
- Stress Tester is a new item that reduces the holder's HP to 1 at the start of battle against a Wild Pokémon. It also guarantees escape from battle. 
- +1 Soda and +5 Soda are items found in any PokéMart that heal for 1 and 5 HP respectively. Use this in combination with Stress Tester to predamage.
- You can press R in the Pokémon Summary to view IVs and EVs. 
- A Psychic in each Pokémon Center will tell you your Pokémon's Hidden Power Type 

Bug Reports, resources, discussion, and a forum to provide feedback can be found on the Cascade White Discord (https://discord.gg/DTQmDVUPgu).

- Red Shards: Seed Bomb, Skitter Smack, Drill Run, Bounce, Signal Beam, Iron Head, SKill Swap, Seething Cold, Psychic Noise, Dual Chop, Low Kick, Cross Poison, Fire Punch, Thunder Punch, Ice Punch
- Yellow Shards: Explosion, Self Destruct, Zen Headbutt, Magic Coat, Barrier, Defense Curl, Sweet Scent, Gastro Acid, Iron Tail, Aqua Tail, Earth Power, Psyblade, Foul Play, Focus Punch, Gravity, Follow Me, Protect
