# Cascade White 2

- You can press the START button to use the PokeHelper, which lets you access your PC, heal your Pokemon, pre-apply status, and more.
- You can also change game settings here for stuff like critical hit damage, wild pokemon IVs, and more.
- One of these settings is for animated battle backgrounds. Be wary that not all of these are tested and some visual glitches may occur.
